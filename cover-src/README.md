# The cover picture

`public/cover.jpg` (1200×750, no text) is the board set on a painted
backdrop, made in two parts:

- **The backdrop.** `backdrop.jpg` is a painting of a forest glade with a
  castle and a river, supplied by the game's owner. Its pale right and
  bottom edges are trimmed, and it is scaled to fill the cover, keeping the
  left of the picture so the castle on the right stays clear of the board.
- **The board.** It is shot from the game twice at 2400×1500, once over
  black and once over white, with the scenery left out. The difference
  between the two shots gives each pixel's transparency, so the stones keep
  their soft edges. The cut-out is set a little left of the middle with a
  soft shadow under each stone.
